T Wraps — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-24

T Wraps ("the app") lets you design wrap images for your car's 3D visualization. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data stored on your device

  • Your designs, exported wrap images and settings are stored only on your device (app storage and a local SQLite database). They are not uploaded to us.
  • Photos you add to a design are copied into the app's private storage. The app only accesses photos you pick in the system photo picker.
  • Exported images are saved to your photo library, shared, or copied to a folder only when you choose to do so.

We do not operate servers and do not collect your designs, photos or files.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and interactions with ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and in US states with applicable privacy laws, the app asks for your consent choices through Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Third-party content

Vehicle templates and example wraps are based on Tesla's public custom-wraps repository. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Inc.
